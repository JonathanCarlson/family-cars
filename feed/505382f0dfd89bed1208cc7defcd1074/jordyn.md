## How to read this

- Powertrain is resolved from the **VIN** via the NHTSA vPIC database, never
  from the model name. Model names lie: a "Hyundai Ioniq" may be a hybrid, a
  plug-in hybrid, or a battery EV, and only the VIN distinguishes them.
- Any car whose powertrain could not be VIN-verified is marked ⚠️. Its range
  and fuel costs are assumptions, not facts.
- Reliability is model-year level, triangulated from free public NHTSA data.
  It is NOT a purchased J.D. Power or Consumer Reports score, and it says
  nothing about the condition of the specific car — get a pre-purchase inspection.
